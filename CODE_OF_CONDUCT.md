# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of experience, nationality, disability, identity or background. Be considerate, give constructive feedback, cite evidence and respect different understandings of cultural heritage.

Harassment, threats, hateful language, sexual harassment, deliberate misrepresentation, repeated personal attacks and publication of private information are unacceptable. Critique an interpretation or submission without attacking its contributor. Handle historical offensive language with context and care.

This code applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other official community spaces. Repository maintainers enforce it. They may edit or remove contributions, warn participants, restrict participation temporarily, or block repeated or serious violations, considering context and impact.

For a public concern that can safely be discussed, contact the maintainer through a repository issue without repeating harmful material or private information. For a sensitive report, use GitHub's reporting tools or a private contact route the future maintainer explicitly provides. This starter repository has no staffed reporting inbox; do not post private allegations or personal data in public issues. Maintainers should document a private reporting route before opening a larger community.

Enforcement should be fair, proportionate and respectful of confidentiality. This is an original project policy, not a claim of endorsement by an external organization.
