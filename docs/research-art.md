# Art and material-culture research

Checked: 2026-10-30. This batch contains 28 individually checked institutional records.

## Method and rights interpretation

Each included object was fetched directly from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Collection API. Inclusion required `isPublicDomain` to be `true` and a nonempty `primaryImage`. Item-level title, date, attribution and image format were inspected in those JSON records. Human-readable catalog pages were also consulted through web retrieval for selected objects, including the Great Wave, Book of Hours, Shiva Nataraja, Shah Jahan album folio, Mihrab, Ethiopian healing scroll, Qur'an bifolia, Moche vessel, Korean maebyeong, and West African equestrian figures.

The [Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources) explains that eligible images of works the institution believes public domain are made available under CC0. The [image FAQ](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/frequently-asked-questions-image-and-data-resources) distinguishes these images from restricted material. These pages were opened and read. The data labels the underlying work Public Domain and records the CC0 image-policy basis in `rights_evidence`. The API is an institutional statement, not an independent universal copyright ruling. JPEG denotes the institutional API's advertised image extension; image binaries were not downloaded or pixel-tested. Some manuscripts are represented by selected openings, not a complete downloadable facsimile.

## Exclusions and limitations

- Claude Monet, Garden at Sainte-Adresse, object 437133: inspected official API returned `isPublicDomain: false` and no primary image; excluded despite the age of the painting.
- Marshall Islands Rebbilib navigational chart, object 311297: official API returned `isPublicDomain: false` and no primary image; webpage also states that the image cannot be downloaded. Excluded despite its exceptional relevance and the age of the object.
- The selected objects span cultural origins in East, South and West Asia, North, East and West Africa, Europe, and the Americas. All are held by one US institution; geographic origin must not be confused with holding-institution diversity. No Oceanian object survived this batch's rights check.
- Metadata can change. Dates and attributions are retained as catalogued, including approximate periods and uncertainty. A null creator means no individual maker was supplied, not that the work lacks a cultural origin.
- Cultural authority, colonial acquisition history, sacred use and community expectations are distinct from copyright. A public-domain image label does not resolve those issues.
