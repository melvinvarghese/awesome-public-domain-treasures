# Science, maps, and engineering research

Reviewed 2026-10-30. Twenty entries were selected only after primary institutional records were retrieved with an explicit item-level Public Domain Mark, Public domain statement, or No Copyright – United States statement. Descriptions are original paraphrases. Dates and attribution follow the records; shortened display titles are editorial.

## Method and limits

- Retrieved pages through the web research tool. No automated check establishes legal status.
- e-rara records expose item-specific Public Domain Mark terms and IIIF manifests. Only IIIF is listed as a format here; bulk downloads were not downloaded or tested. Institutions ask for scholarly credit and notification; consult the current terms.
- Wellcome records were read in full, including their item-specific Public Domain Mark; only online viewing was confirmed. Its official content.www subdomain was used where the main hostname returned bot protection.
- BHL item records were read in full, including Copyright & Usage and listed download formats; download payloads were not tested.
- Texas A&M records were read in full, including the United States-only rights declaration, descriptive metadata, TIFF resolution options, and IIIF links.
- Region records both production context and subject where they differ. A European map of Africa is not evidence of African authorship. The science subset remains European-heavy; this is a documented editorial limitation.

## Candidates excluded or replaced

- Multiple Library of Congress book records (including An atlas of astronomy, Engineering book, and steam-engine books) had explicit public-domain text in indexed official results but returned HTTP 403 when opened. They were excluded in favor of fully retrieved records.
- A Wellcome Harvey 1628 PDF carried a PDM cover but displayed restricted-page notices; excluded rather than presenting it as a verified complete downloadable book.
- Vanderbilt’s Burritt atlas exhibit record stated Public Domain but exposed no digital files in retrieved text; replaced by Bayer’s fully documented e-rara atlas.
- Wellcome’s Vesalius facsimile image and Redouté Exocarpos item lacked rights text in the fetched main record despite indexed PDM claims; excluded.
- UWM celestial pages returned JavaScript-only shells; excluded rather than relying solely on search snippets.
- No failed candidate is included in the main collection.
